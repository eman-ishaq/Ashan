12 JANUARY 2024 · 7:00 PM

Ek accidental snap

Mujhe naya naya mobile mila tha aur Ashan ko bhi Karachi aaye zyada waqt nahi hua tha. Maghrib ka waqt tha, main noodles bana kar apni friend ko snap send kar rahi thi. Light chali gayi to Wi-Fi band ho gaya, maine mobile data on kiya aur snap send kar di. Pata nahi kaise, woh Ashan ko bhi chali gayi — aur bas, yahin se hamari baat shuru hui. Ashan ka message aaya, “Assalamualaikum.” Phir hum dono ne ek doosre se poocha, “Aap kaun?” Ashan ne kaha, “Aap wohi jo main samajh raha hoon?” Maine kaha, “Aap wohi jo main samajh rahi hoon?” Aur phir dono ne kaha, “Ji, main bhi wohi.” Aaj bhi sochun to is chhoti si baat par hansi aa jati hai.

JANUARY – MAY 2024

Roz ki baatein

Us din ke baad hum roz baat karne lage. Koi aisa din yaad nahi aata jab baat na hui ho. Ashan apni doodh-badar wali shop par kaam mein busy hote, lekin jaise hi do minute milte, mujhe message kar dete. Dheere dheere woh hamare din ka hissa ban gaya. 8 May 2024 ko mera Urdu ka paper tha aur main raat ko tayari kar rahi thi. Baaton baaton mein maine rough page par “Eman” likha aur beech mein Ashan ke naam bhi chhupa diye. Maine photo bhej kar kaha ke naam dhoondh lein; agar dhoondh liya to main unki har baat sunungi. Ashan ne naam dhoondh liya, lekin jab maine poocha ab kya kehna hai, to bole “abhi nahi, phir kabhi.” Maine zid ki to unhon ne kaha ke unki dosti aage barhti ja rahi hai. Main do minute chup rahi, phir kaha ke mere Ammi Abu jo faisla karein ge, main wohi karungi. Ashan ne kaha ke woh sab ko mana lein ge. Us waqt woh baat pending reh gayi, lekin mere dil mein bohat kuch badalne laga.

9–10 JULY 2024

Pehli mulaqat aur chai

June mein ghar wale Punjab chale gaye aur main Urooj ke saath Karachi mein thi. 9 July ki raat Ashan ghar aaye to Urooj so rahi thi. Ashan chai banane aaye, to maine kaha, “Main bana deti hoon.” Main bohat nervous thi aur sharam bhi aa rahi thi. Chai banayi aur Ashan wahin kharay ho kar peete rahe. Hum der tak baatein karte rahe, haath pakra aur apni heights bhi match ki. Woh hamari pehli mulaqat thi. Agli subah 10 July ko bhi hum mile. Phir 24 July ko raat ke teen baje mile aur maine Ashan ke haath par kiss bhi ki. 26 July ko bhi mulaqat hui. Yeh chhote chhote pal mere liye bohat khaas ban gaye.

OCTOBER 2024

Ek poora mahina, bohat si yaadein

October mein Ammi aur Aliza dobara Punjab gayin. Is baar main aur Ashan lagbhag poora mahina roz milte rahe. Woh waqt bohat pyara tha — baatein, hansna, saath waqt guzarna. Beech mein do teen aur mulaqatein bhi hoti rahin. Har din apni jagah ek yaad ban gaya.

31 MARCH – 4 APRIL 2025

Chhoti birthday, badi khushi

31 March ko ghar wale Punjab chale gaye, lekin mere 9th class ke papers ki wajah se main Karachi mein reh gayi. Ashan bhi kuch din yahin thay. 1 April ko hum mile aur 2 April ko Ashan ne meri chhoti si birthday celebrate ki. Chhoti thi, lekin mere liye bohat achhi thi. 3 April ko main, Ashan aur Urooj Imtiaz gaye; bohat maza aaya. 4 April ko Ashan ko Punjab jana tha, kyunki unki behan ki shaadi thi.

2 MAY 2025

Ocean Mall wali mulaqat

Mera aakhri paper 2 May ko tha. Friends ne Ocean Mall ka plan banaya. Maine Ashan ko bataya to unhon ne kaha, free ho kar batana, main bhi aa jaunga. Main friends ke saath ek do ghante rahi; phir woh chali gayin aur Ashan aa gaye. Humne movie dekhi, bohat baatein ki aur khoob enjoy kiya. Us din ki khushi aaj bhi yaad hai.

24 MAY – 13 JUNE 2026

Punjab ka safar

24 May ko hum sab family ke saath Punjab gaye. Train mein family ke saamne khul kar baat nahi kar sakte thay, is liye aankhon ke ishare aur messages se baatein hoti rahin. Ashan ne mujhe Sting bhi la kar di. Jhung/Mail pohnch kar family ke saath milna, cousins ke saath rehna, chand raat ko sab ka ek jaisi churiyan pehenna aur Eid par ghoomna — sab bohat yaadgaar tha. Ek raat meri tabiyat bohat kharab ho gayi aur BP low ho gaya. Sab so rahe thay, Ashan jaag rahe thay. Unhon ne paani diya, sar, kamar, haath aur paon dabaye aur haath wale pankhay se hawa di. Un dinon unhon ne mere liye chocolate bhi la kar di thi. Us waqt unka mera khayal rakhna main nahi bhool sakti. Family ki dawat mein dono ghar walon ne saath bohat kaam kiya, khana serve kiya, baatein ki aur bartan dhotay hue bhi itna hansay ke thakan mehsoos nahi hui. Ek din hamare blue kapray bhi match ho gaye aur behnon ne mazaak banaya. Kasur ke Funville aur Baba Kamal Chishti ke mazar par bhi gaye. Ashan ne mujhe ship wala jhoola teen baar khilaya, cup wala jhoola bhi, aur mazar par bracelet diya. Wapas aane se pehle station par Ashan ne mujh se haath milaya. Train late thi aur mausam bhi kharab tha, magar sab se mushkil baat wapas aana tha — kyunki hum sab ne saath bohat achha waqt guzara tha. Hum 13 June ko Karachi pohnche.

JUNE – AUGUST 2026

Woh waqt jo aasaan nahi tha

Karachi aane ke baad 20 June se hamari laraiyan shuru ho gayin. Main ne kabhi nahi socha tha ke ek din apne munh se Ashan ko “rishta bhej dein” jaisi baat kahungi. Lekin main pareshan thi aur mere dil mein bohat si fikrein thi. Hamari laraiyan taqreeban do mahine chalti rahin. Ashan 28 August ko Karachi aaye, tab bhi baat foran theek nahi hui; baad mein humne cheezein suljhane ki koshish ki. Mujhe bas yeh samjhana hai ke mera maqsad kabhi Ashan ko tension dena ya un par bojh dalna nahi. Main chahti hoon ke mera naam kisi aur ke saath na jure, siwaye unke. Main jaanti hoon ke abhi hum dono ko bohat kuch karna hai, struggle karni hai aur waqt dena hai. Main sirf chahti hoon ke jab munasib ho, family ko bata kar hamare baare mein baat shuru ho sake. Baqi sab apne waqt par.

Ashan, yeh aap ke liye hai.

Ashan, Kabhi kabhi mujhe khud hairani hoti hai ke ek accidental snap se shuru hone wali baat meri zindagi ki itni khaas kahani kaise ban gayi. 12 January 2024 ki woh shaam mujhe hamesha yaad rahegi. Mujhe hamari roz ki baatein, aapka busy hone ke bawajood waqt nikal kar message karna, woh pehli chai, woh mulaqatein, Punjab ki yaadein, family ke saath hansna aur woh chhoti chhoti cheezein yaad rehti hain jo shayad doosron ke liye aam hon, lekin mere liye bohat khaas hain. Main jaanti hoon ke har waqt sab kuch aasaan nahi hota. Humse ghaltiyan bhi hoti hain aur kabhi baat samjhana mushkil ho jata hai. Lekin meri baat ka maqsad aapko pareshan karna nahi. Main bas chahti hoon ke meri feelings ko samjha jaye aur hum izzat, samajh aur sukoon ke saath apni zindagi ke faislay karein. Aaj aapke birthday par meri dua hai ke Allah aapko sehat, kamyabi, barkat aur dil ka sukoon de. Aapki mehnat rang laaye, aapke raste aasaan hon aur aap hamesha khush rahein. Happy Birthday, Ashan. Aap mere liye bohat special hain — apne usi alag, apne se ehsaas ke saath. ♥
